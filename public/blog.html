BPSU Announces New Academic Programs for 2024

Dr. Marcus Evans
• October 28, 2025 • 5 min read

Bataan Peninsula State University (BPSU) is excited to announce the launch of several new academic programs for the academic year 2024. These initiatives aim to broaden opportunities for students and meet the evolving needs of the local and global workforce.

Overview of New Programs

  • BS in Artificial Intelligence — focusing on applied machine learning, robotics, and data ethics.
  • BS in Environmental Sustainability — integrating green technology and policy studies.
  • BS in Creative Media Arts — merging technology with digital storytelling.

These programs were developed after extensive consultation with industry partners, ensuring that graduates will be equipped with skills relevant to current market demands.

"BPSU continues to evolve to match the rapid pace of innovation while keeping education accessible and inclusive," said Dr. Marcus Evans.

Enrollment Information

Enrollment for the new programs will begin in May 2024. Interested students can visit the BPSU Registrar’s Office or check the official BPSU Bulletin website for updates.

For inquiries, contact the Academic Affairs Office or follow the official BPSU social media channels for announcements.
